# Mathrix Production Readiness — Not Ready (updated after remediation + remaining-phases pass)

## Verdict: **Not ready** (unchanged), but the reason has narrowed considerably

The original verdict was driven by a live P0 (DEF-008) plus two P1s (DEF-010, DEF-013) and an unenforced review gap (DEF-003). **DEF-008, DEF-010, and DEF-013 are now fixed and verified** (see `MATHRIX_DEFECT_REGISTER.csv`'s `status` column for reproduction/verification detail on each). The remaining structural blocker is narrower but still real:

- **DEF-003 (P1, fields added, not enforced)**: nothing in the serving path checks `reviewStatus` before serving a lesson. The fields now exist (`lessonId`, `contentVersion`, `modelVersion`, `promptVersion`, `reviewStatus`, `teacherReviewer`, `sourceReferences`) and are stamped on every generated/cached lesson, but every one of the 437+ cached lessons is `reviewStatus: "unreviewed"` and nothing stops them being served. This was a deliberate scope decision (see `MATHRIX_REMEDIATION_PLAN.md`) — enforcing it now, with zero lessons ever reviewed, would brick the product rather than fix it. Building the actual review workflow is separate, larger work.

**Since that verdict was written, the remaining audit phases (2, 5/6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12) were actually run** (see `MATHRIX_EXECUTIVE_AUDIT.md`'s "Remaining-phases findings" section). Headline results: every authorisation/IDOR boundary tested held (Phase 10); the prompt-injection defense held live against a real attack string (Phase 8); the whiteboard cursor/target architecture was confirmed spatially correct in 40/40 live samples (Phase 6); but a real WCAG 2.2 AA gap was found (28 axe-core violations, including a critical `button-name` failure — Phase 9), a real safeguarding defense-in-depth gap was found (DEF-019 — a harmful-content phrasing bypassed the intended pre-LLM safety redirect, though no harmful content actually reached the pupil — Phase 8), and curriculum-coverage verification remains a bounded 2-of-28-topics spot-check, not a full matrix (Phase 2). None of DEF-014–019 (the six new findings from this pass) have been fixed. This verdict would not change even if DEF-014–019 were all fixed, because DEF-003's review-gate gap is structural, not a bug with a specific repro — but DEF-015 (critical-impact accessibility) and DEF-019 (child-safety defense-in-depth) are both real, additional reasons a "Ready" verdict would be premature right now, independent of DEF-003.
